# ✨ Welcome to *Space

***Space** is a collaborative co-working space where ideas flourish, projects come to life, and community thrives.

🌐 **[Visit our website](https://starspace.group)** | 💬 **[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/b7cjNQ7zB6)** (our main community space)

## 🌟 About Us

*Space is more than just a co-working space—it's a community of makers, builders, creators, and innovators. We believe in the power of collaboration, open source, and shared knowledge. This organization serves as the digital hub for our community, bringing together projects, resources, and people who want to build something amazing together.

## 🚀 Get Involved

We encourage everyone to contribute, collaborate, and build with us! Here's how you can get started:

### 🐙 GitHub
- **Explore our repositories**: Browse through our projects and find something that interests you
- **Contribute**: Found a bug? Have an idea? Submit an issue or pull request!
- **Start your own project**: Have an idea? Create a repository under our organization and let the community help you build it
- **Join discussions**: Participate in repository discussions and share your insights

### 💬 Discord
**[Join our Discord](https://discord.gg/b7cjNQ7zB6)** — our main community space where you can connect with other members in real-time:
- Get help with projects
- Share ideas and get feedback
- Collaborate on new initiatives
- Stay updated with community events

### 🤝 Other Ways to Contribute
- **Documentation**: Help improve our docs and make them more accessible
- **Code Review**: Review pull requests and help maintain code quality
- **Mentorship**: Share your expertise and help others learn
- **Events**: Organize or participate in workshops, hackathons, and meetups
- **Spread the word**: Tell others about *Space and what we're building

## 🌈 Community Values

- **Openness**: We embrace open source and transparent collaboration
- **Inclusivity**: Everyone is welcome, regardless of skill level or background
- **Innovation**: We encourage experimentation and creative problem-solving
- **Support**: We help each other grow and succeed
- **Respect**: We treat everyone with kindness and professionalism

## 🎯 What We're Building

Our community is actively working on several open source projects—both those that help the community and those that support *Space directly:

### Current Focus
- **Hermes** (eCommerce Platform): Our main project—an open source eCommerce platform currently in active development

### Coming Soon
- **DAO & Token System**: We're planning to launch a Decentralized Autonomous Organization (DAO) that enables members to participate in *Space governance:
  - **SpaceTime Tokens**: Earned by contributing time to *Space
  - **SpaceMoney Tokens**: Earned by contributing monetary funds to *Space
  - Token-weighted voting system where proposal types determine which tokens carry more weight in decision-making

### Additional Projects
- [Apollo](https://github.com/starspacegroup/apollo)
- [Dashboard](https://github.com/starspacegroup/dashboard)
- [SpaceTime Clock](https://github.com/starspacegroup/spacetime-clock)

## 🏢 Our Vision

Our ultimate goal is to fund *Space through our open source projects and community initiatives, generating enough revenue to purchase real estate—ideally commercial or mixed-use live-work space. This will create a permanent physical home for our community where members can collaborate, build, and thrive together in person.
